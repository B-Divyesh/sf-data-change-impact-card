Policy / effective 2026-08-28

Privacy

Data Change Impact Card is private by default. The CLI reads only the files you name, performs analysis on your machine, and writes only to standard output or the output path you provide.

What we collect

Nothing. The CLI and this documentation site contain no analytics, telemetry, cookies, advertising, accounts, or tracking pixels. The site makes no third-party runtime requests.

Your manifests

Manifest and change contents are never uploaded by the software. Redacted export is available for sharing, but you are responsible for reviewing an artifact before sending it elsewhere.

Offline cache

The documentation site may save its public pages and assets in your browser cache so they remain available offline. This contains no personal or project data and can be removed through your browser’s site-data controls.
